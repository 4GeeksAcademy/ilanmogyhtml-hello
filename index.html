01 / About me

Un poquito
sobre mí.

“All I need is my guitarra, a beach and love.”

I’m Sofia Villarosa, a Spanish singer, songwriter, and actress. I grew up surrounded by the soul of flamenco, the warmth of long summer evenings, and the melodies that make a place feel like home. Today I weave those memories into songs about love, longing, and everything we sometimes struggle to say aloud. My music moves between English and Spanish, but it always comes from the same place: the heart.

02 / Career

The
long play.

  1. Where it all began

    Flamenco in her bones, melodies in her head, and stories she couldn't keep to herself. Sofia found her voice somewhere between Spanish roots and the sounds that carried her beyond them.

  2. Between two worlds

    Blending the raw emotion of flamenco with the freedom of modern pop, Sofia began shaping a sound of her own - intimate, restless, and unapologetically honest. Two languages, one unmistakable voice.

  3. Songs without borders

    From the quiet moments of songwriting to the electricity of a live stage, Sofia is building a musical world that travels across borders, bringing every story back to where it belongs: the heart.
